ABOUT THE INSTITUTE

city College of Arts, Commerce & Science, virar, is affiliated to the University of Mumbai. Founded with the vision of providing quality education, the college has grown into a premier institution offering traditional and professional courses including B.A., B.Com and B.Sc. The college is committed to holistic development of students, nurturing both academic excellence and co-curricular activities including sports.

The Sports Department at city College actively promotes a culture of physical fitness and sportsmanship. Students are encouraged to participate in inter-college tournaments, university-level competitions and national events across a wide range of sports disciplines.


VISION, MISSION & OBJECTIVES


🎯 VISION
To become a centre of excellence in education and sports, nurturing students into well-rounded individuals with strong ethical values, competitive spirit, and a commitment to lifelong physical and mental well-being.
🚀 MISSION
  • To provide excellent sports infrastructure and facilities for students.
  • To promote sports culture and physical fitness across all disciplines.
  • To encourage participation in inter-collegiate, university and national level competitions.
  • To build sportsmanship, team spirit and leadership through sports activities.
  • To identify and nurture talented athletes for higher-level competitions.
📋 OBJECTIVES
  • Promote physical fitness
  • Organise inter-college tournaments
  • Develop team leadership
  • Build sports discipline
  • Support athlete development
  • Manage sports events efficiently
  • Track performance & statistics
  • Foster community engagement
