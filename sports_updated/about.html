About City College Sports

Games are sometimes played purely for entertainment, sometimes for achievement or reward as well. They can be played alone, in teams, or online; by amateurs or by professionals. The Sports Department at SDT Kalani College provides a comprehensive platform for all sports-related activities, events, registrations, and performance tracking.


🏃 Athletics
Track & field events including sprints, relays, long jump, high jump and more. Open to all students.
⚽ Team Sports
Football, Cricket, Basketball, Volleyball and Kabaddi – inter-college and university level competitions.
🏸 Indoor Sports
Badminton, Table Tennis, Chess, Carrom – available year round in our well-equipped indoor sports hall.
